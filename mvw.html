Music Visualisation Workshop
The Music Visualization Workshop explores how sound has been translated into image, from early pre-computer experiments to today's digital tools. The first part traces this history, before and after the arrival of computers; the second is hands-on, with participants creating live visuals using Resolume Arena, cameras, motors and lights.
The workshop has been held at the Bauhaus-Universität Weimar and at the Goethe-Institut in Jakarta and Ulaanbaatar, bringing together artists, musicians and students from very different backgrounds. In Jakarta, it included a group of deaf young women, opening a new perspective on music as something that can be seen and felt, not only heard.
