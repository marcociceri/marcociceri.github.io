Marco Ciceri
Those Days Will Never Return

Single-channel video, 5'27", 16:9, LED screen or projection.

Music: Marco Scipione

The video is shot up close, where the image slips away from ordinary composition and becomes distorted, in a slow, intense, obscure world where scale and material lose their edges. It is an investigation into the materiality of fire: not flame as spectacle, but fire as matter, observed as it enters the charcoal, consumes it and transforms it into light, heat and ash. The video follows the four acts of the track: it opens on an abstract detail of charcoal. The frame pulls back to reveal it as landscape, then fire ignites across it. What remains, in the end, is embers, the afterimage of everything before, still glowing but sombrely fading away.

The video was commissioned together with the cover artwork by Marco Scipione as a video clip for the single 'Those Days Will Never Return' out on Cassis Records.
